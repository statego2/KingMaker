# Chat playtest 001 — the anonymous voice

**Date:** 2026-10-08  
**Status:** exploratory chat prototype; scene and branch details below are **not yet implemented in the browser game**.  
**Related:** `docs/CHAT_FIRST_PLAYTEST_WORKFLOW.md`, `docs/STORY_BIBLE_V2_CINEMATIC_REDESIGN.md`, `src/content.js`.

## Owner direction

The owner wants to play and co-design KINGMAKER in the conversation until it becomes genuinely fun and worthwhile. The AI presents the game and simulates consequences here; the agreed story, rules and game state are then developed in GitHub. The chat-first workflow takes priority for creative iteration over polishing the existing front end at this stage. This does not retire the browser prototype or imply it already supports free-text play.

## Observed player choices

- The opening is an anonymous voicemail: «Με έχουν μετρήσει στην κυβέρνηση. Δεν έχω υποσχεθεί την ψήφο μου σε κανέναν».
- The player asked Λέα to check which votes were called secure, without sharing the voicemail.
- Λέα found 119 personally confirmed and three marked together with them despite no direct yes.
- The player let Μάρα's call ring while Λέα checked the provenance of Νίκο's entry. The recorded phrase was «Θα ακούσω την πρότασή σας», represented by someone else as agreement.
- The player then chose to investigate the other two rather than report immediately.
- At **07:27** in this experimental branch: one of those two gave a direct new yes (**120 confirmed**); the other has a condition about keeping a regional hospital (**one conditional**); Νίκο remains uncommitted. Μάρα asks for a report and immediate recommendation by 07:30.
- A second message from the unknown caller says they are near the building and want to know who spoke for them. The caller's identity has **not** been established in-play. Λέα still has not heard the voicemail.

**Pending player prompt:** Μάρα asks, «Πες μου τι βρήκες — και τι προτείνεις να κάνουμε τώρα». The owner interrupted the game at this point to establish the production workflow. Resume from here if they choose; do not auto-answer for the player.

## Design observations and implementation candidates

- Existing feedback from the owner: introduce the plot gradually like a well-designed film; use few, understandable names and plain Greek; avoid a technical or obviously graded opening.
- This chat run made investigation an actual action with time cost and a changed information state before commitment. Keep it as a candidate for the Gold Chapter 1 loop.
- The session has not yet proved whether the sequence is fun over multiple scenes. Ask for critique naturally during play, record concrete friction, and iterate.
- The browser's `C01_S01` and v0.3 engine have a more constrained choice model. Implementing these free-text-style investigative branches needs authored action/state contracts, tests and save compatibility; do not paste this improvisation into canonical content as if it were already validated.
