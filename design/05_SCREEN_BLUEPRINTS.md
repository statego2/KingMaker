# KINGMAKER — Screen Blueprints

These are composition blueprints, not implementation layouts.

---

## A. Chapter opening

Top 15%
Act label
Chapter number
Location / time

Middle 55%
Key art or restrained environmental motion
Chapter title in serif
One sentence premise

Bottom 30%
Primary pressure
One unresolved thread carried from previous chapter
Enter briefing

Purpose:
ceremony without excessive cinematic interruption.

---

## B. Morning briefing

Top
Date
Current role
Current access level

Section 1
THREE THINGS THAT CHANGED

Each item:
headline
source
confidence
why it matters

Section 2
CLOCKS

Only active deadlines.

Section 3
PEOPLE TO WATCH

2–4 actor portraits with change markers.

Bottom
Open Decision Agenda

---

## C. Character dossier

Hero area
Portrait
Name
Role
Affiliation

Primary strip
Access
Trust
Respect
Dependency

Not as arcade bars.
Use compact analytical indicators.

Tabs:
Known
History
Network
Promises
Sources

Known tab:
Objectives
Likely fears
Observed values
Current position

Important:
separate player inference from confirmed fact.

---

## D. Network view

Canvas:
nodes arranged by current selected lens.

Top:
Lens selector.

Bottom:
selected node detail.

Tap edge:
relationship provenance and recent events.

Long press node:
pin for comparison.

Never show all metrics at once.

---

## E. Decision room

Top:
Decision
time
owner
commitment level

Body:
Situation
Known
Unknown
Objective

Stakeholder strip

Option stack

Each option:
verb
one-sentence action
cost
timing
reversibility

Bottom:
Inspect
Commit

For constitutional decisions:
commit becomes deliberate hold.

---

## F. Consequence screen

No giant score.

Start with:
WHAT HAPPENED

Then:
REACTIONS

Then:
WHAT CHANGED

Then optional:
WHY THE GAME JUDGED THIS DECISION AS IT DID

Separate:
Decision Quality
Outcome Quality

Never collapse them.

---

## G. Crisis board

Used mainly in Act V.

Top:
national clock
current role

Center:
3–5 crisis lanes maximum.

Each lane:
state
trend
next threshold
responsible institution
your current intervention

Bottom:
resources
communications
priority order

The screen should feel calm under pressure.

---

## H. Legacy screen

Act VI only.

Primary comparison:
Personal Centrality
Institutional Capacity

Then:
Succession
Rule Neutrality
Resilience
Optionality
Public Legitimacy
Concentrated Dependencies

Archive milestones appear behind the metrics as causal evidence.

This screen should make the player ask:
Did I build a system, or merely become the system?
