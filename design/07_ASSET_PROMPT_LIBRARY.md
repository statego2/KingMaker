# KINGMAKER — Asset Prompt Library

These are art-production briefs, not runtime prompts. They exist to keep future human or generative production visually consistent.

## Global portrait brief

Prestige political-thriller editorial portrait, Republic of Lydria in 2032, credible contemporary professional wardrobe, naturalistic directional office light, restrained palette, institutional environment, subtle tension in expression, realistic skin texture, no glamour retouching, no neon, no military cosplay, no villain coding, no text in image, generous negative space for mobile UI, cinematic but believable, consistent facial identity across variants.

### Negative constraints

Avoid:
cyberpunk, hologram overload, tactical gear unless canonically required, exaggerated expression, superhero lighting, luxury-fashion campaign, obvious left/right political symbolism, real-world flags, text artifacts.

## Act key art briefs

### Act I — The Outsider

A single analyst at the edge of a long briefing table, powerful figures mostly out of frame, one illuminated folder and a partial map of Lydria, composition emphasizes peripheral access and incomplete information.

### Act II — The Operator

A desk becoming a system: documents, project lines and first network connections linking ministries, labour, infrastructure and finance; human presence implied, not heroic.

### Act III — The Kingmaker

A dense relationship network over Velis where several institutional paths converge around an empty central space; avoid literal crown, imply centrality through geometry.

### Act IV — The Counterplayer

Two overlapping intelligence models of the same political event, conflicting annotations and source trails, one network edge subtly redirected, visual theme: being observed while observing.

### Act V — The System

National strategic systems under synchronized pressure: energy, finance, labour, maritime and infrastructure represented as coherent operational layers around one narrowing time window; intense but not chaotic.

### Act VI — Legacy

Quiet early light over Velis, an empty institutional chair, archived decisions on a table, a succession network continuing beyond the absent player, visual theme: durability after personal power.

## Document brief

Government / intelligence / corporate documents should be visually authored objects:
- provenance visible,
- hierarchy clean,
- no decorative filler,
- timestamps and classification areas designed for localization,
- high mobile readability,
- print-like materiality even on screen.

---

## Consistency workflow

For recurring characters:
1. approve one canonical face,
2. lock age, facial structure, hair, skin tone and signature wardrobe language,
3. generate alternates from the canonical reference,
4. reject identity drift even when individual image quality is high.

Consistency beats novelty.
