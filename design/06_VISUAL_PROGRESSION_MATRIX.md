# KINGMAKER — Visual Progression Matrix

The interface should mature with the player's political role. Progression is not a skin swap; it changes what the player can perceive, compare and control.

| Dimension | Act I Outsider | Act II Operator | Act III Kingmaker | Act IV Counterplayer | Act V System | Act VI Legacy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Information access | fragments | curated feeds | cross-institution view | contested intelligence | national system view | historical / institutional view |
| Main visual object | document | project dashboard | network | provenance graph | crisis board | archive / succession map |
| Network visibility | sparse | partial | dense | ambiguous / adversarial | operational | structural |
| Color intensity | very restrained | slightly richer | authority brass rises | indigo / ambiguity | amber / oxide pressure | desaturated calm |
| Motion | document arrival | system activation | connection / rearrangement | contradiction / reclassification | compression / countdown | slow historical reveal |
| Player position | receiver | coordinator | broker | target and modeler | orchestrator | architect / risk |
| Main emotional question | What is happening? | What can I move? | Who depends on whom? | Who is modeling me? | What fails first? | What survives me? |

## Progression rule

Never unlock complexity merely because "the player leveled up."

Unlock a surface because:
- they gained access,
- acquired staff,
- built an institution,
- earned trust,
- created a reporting system,
- inherited responsibility,
- or survived enough events to possess historical data.

This lets UI progression become diegetic progression.

## Example

Act I:
The player sees three fragments about Aster Gate.

Act II:
The player sees the project timeline and ministries.

Act III:
The player sees the concession network and dependencies.

Act IV:
The player sees contradictory source trails and adversarial signaling.

Act V:
The player sees how Aster Gate failure propagates through energy, finance, labour and public legitimacy.

Act VI:
The player sees whether the institutional architecture around Aster Gate works without them.
