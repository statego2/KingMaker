# KINGMAKER — Asset Bible

## 1. Asset philosophy

Every asset must belong to the world.

Avoid generic "strategy game" decoration.

A portrait, seal, map or icon should communicate role, provenance or institutional context.

---

## 2. Character portrait package

For each Tier A recurring character:

Required:
- canonical neutral portrait,
- pressure portrait,
- public appearance portrait,
- private / low-light portrait where narratively justified,
- 3 crop formats: 4:5, 1:1, 16:9 contextual.

For Tier B:
- canonical portrait,
- one alternate emotional state,
- same crop support.

Do not generate dozens of expression sprites.
The tone is prestige drama, not visual novel caricature.

### Portrait metadata
character_id
age
role
affiliation
location
wardrobe
lighting
public_mask
current_pressure
do_not_include

## 4. Institution asset package

Per major institution:

- wordmark,
- seal / emblem,
- document header,
- ID / badge treatment,
- presentation / briefing cover,
- signage,
- map marker,
- monochrome stamp variant.

Important:
not every institution needs a literal heraldic crest.

Technology and media organizations can use modern marks.

## 6. Map assets

Base layers:
- national political map,
- physical geography,
- transport network,
- Aster Gate corridor,
- energy network,
- ports,
- digital / data infrastructure,
- regional economy,
- maritime zone.

Dynamic overlays:
- access,
- risk,
- pressure,
- dependency,
- crisis propagation,
- project status.

Do not bake labels into art if they need localization.

## 7. Document assets

Document archetypes:

- presidential note,
- NSO briefing,
- Assembly procedure sheet,
- intelligence note,
- market memo,
- journalist evidence packet,
- corporate proposal,
- diplomatic cable,
- legal ruling,
- emergency order.

Each needs:
- header,
- provenance cue,
- confidence / classification zone,
- signature / approval space,
- version / timestamp area.

Documents are gameplay objects.

---

## 8. Background ambient assets

Use subtle loops:
- city rain behind glass,
- late-night office lights,
- parliamentary hallway movement,
- port cranes,
- market screens,
- coastline haze.

Motion should be nearly subliminal.

Never turn backgrounds into screensavers.

## 10. Act key art concept

Act I:
one person at edge of lit briefing table.

Act II:
multiple documents, map and a single active line connecting institutions.

Act III:
player silhouette absent; network itself forms a crown-like geometry without literal crown imagery.

Act IV:
two overlapping maps / dossiers with conflicting annotations.

Act V:
national systems converging under one clock.

Act VI:
empty chair, archived documents, open horizon over Velis.

---

## 11. Logo direction

Avoid:
literal crown over text,
chess king,
lion crest,
sword,
red-black authoritarian aesthetic.

Preferred:
typographic KINGMAKER mark with a subtle structural motif:
- split vertical axis,
- network crown hidden in negative space,
- gate / arch motif,
- two levels of authority.

Subtitle:
THE GAME OF JUDGMENT

Logo should work:
- in one color,
- at 32 px app icon scale,
- on paper,
- on dark UI.

---

## 12. Asset quality gates

Reject an asset if:
- it looks generically "AI cinematic",
- it uses cyberpunk neon,
- character clothing feels costume-like,
- institutional design is too obviously copied from a real government,
- the image reveals moral alignment through villain coding,
- text is baked into generated imagery,
- map geography contradicts canon,
- portrait age / identity drifts between assets,
- the composition leaves no room for interface use.
